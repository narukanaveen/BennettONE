Campus Wi-Fi Information

Use the following written instructions to connect your laptop or mobile phone to Bennett University Wi-Fi networks:

1. Academic Blocks (A, B, M, N, P, Q): Connect to Bennett-Student and sign in with your student enrollment ID and portal password.

2. Hostel Blocks (C & D): Select your hostel floor Wi-Fi network and log in using your allotted hostel Wi-Fi credentials.

Tip: If Wi-Fi fails to connect after a password reset, click "Forget Network" and enter your updated password again.
